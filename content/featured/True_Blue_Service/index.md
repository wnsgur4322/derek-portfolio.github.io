True Blue Service is a cloud-based field service management platform designed to streamline operations for service technicians and companies.  
It enables teams to manage jobs, customers, and technicians efficiently through a modern, intuitive web interface.

Built with **Next.js** and **Tailwind CSS** on the frontend and a **Go-powered backend** running on **AWS**, the platform features secure data handling with **PostgreSQL RDS**, scalable architecture via **ALB**, and static content delivery through **CloudFront**.  

The goal of True Blue Service is to simplify field operations while ensuring reliability, scalability, and performance for real-world service businesses.
