Humix is a dynamic video platform built to empower creators and digital publishers through seamless video creation, distribution, and monetization.  
It provides an end-to-end ecosystem for hosting, embedding, and analyzing videos — enabling publishers to grow engagement and revenue efficiently.

The platform features a custom **Video.js player**, **AI-driven video categorization** using **ChatGPT**, and intelligent video matching powered by **AWS SageMaker**.  
On the backend, **Go microservices** and **MySQL** handle large-scale data processing and analytics, while **AWS CloudFront** ensures fast, global video delivery.

Humix represents the mission of combining video technology and AI to foster creativity, scalability, and smarter content engagement across the web.
